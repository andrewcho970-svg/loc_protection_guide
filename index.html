Platinum Plan — Models on a 16K Oil Change Schedule

12 MTH / 16K — Service #2

YesOil Change

Oil and filter service & tire rotation. Lubricate locks, latches and hinges; inspect lights, horn and wipers; check coolant, brake, transmission & washer fluid levels; inspect drive belts; inspect transfer case & differentials for leaks.

18 MTH / 24K — Service #1*

NoOil Change

Inspect & adjust all fluid levels; reset maintenance reminder light; remove wheels and visually inspect brake pads, calipers, rotors, brake lines & hoses*; remove & inspect cabin and engine air filters (replace if needed, cost to guest); inspect wiper blades; check installation of driver's floor mat.

24 MTH / 32K — Service #3

YesOil Change

Includes Service #1 & #2, plus: battery service & comprehensive electrical charging system evaluation; audible valve clearance inspection; adjust belt tension if required; check steering gear linkage & shock absorbers for looseness, damage or leakage; inspect ball joints, dust covers, driveshaft, steering rack boots, chassis nuts & bolts.

30 MTH / 40K — Service #1*

NoOil Change

Same as 18 MTH/24K Service #1 above.

36 MTH / 48K — Service #2

YesOil Change

Same as 12 MTH/16K Service #2 above, plus brake fluid replacement required.

*Certain driving habits and specific climatic/regional operating conditions may necessitate a "brake service" (removal, cleaning, deglazing and lubrication of all brake system components & adjustment of parking brake) to protect brake components from premature wear. If your vehicle requires a brake service, additional charges will apply.

Exclusions — The above maintenance services do not include any items that require periodic replacement, such as engine air filters, cabin air filters, wiper blades, belts, hoses or brake services. As well as any services that are not on the Lexus Scheduled Maintenance Plan.

Source: "Included Maintenance Benefits Program," lexus.ca/en/know-your-lexus/coverage/extra-care-protection. **The complimentary 3-year maintenance program includes only the services listed above (3 years up to 48,000 km) unless stated under Exclusions. Section 03

Lexus LCPO Protection — Coverage Overview

A snapshot of the component groups covered under standard Certified Pre-Owned coverage versus the Certified Pre-Owned Upgrade coverage.

Certified Pre-Owned Coverage

  • Engine — block, heads, oil pan, mounts, manifold, harmonic balancer, flywheel, timing components, valve covers, oil/water pump, fuel pump, ECU
  • Manual/Automatic Transmission or Transaxle — case, clutch cover, oil pan, mounts, cooler & lines, torque converter
  • Front/Rear Wheel Drive — final drive housing, CV joints, front hub, axle housing, driveshaft
  • Four Wheel Drive — transfer case, differential assemblies, front hub & spindle
  • Safety Restraints — seatbelts, airbags
  • Seals / Gaskets / Fluid / Filters

Certified Pre-Owned Upgrade Coverage

(In addition to all Certified Pre-Owned coverage)

  • Engine — camshaft cover, exhaust manifold, timing belt tensioner, radiator, thermostat, starter, injection pump
  • Heating/AC — all A/C components, blower motors, electronic heater control
  • Steering & Electrical System — power steering, door locks, seat/sunroof motors, wiring harness
  • High Tech Components — suspension, transmission and ABS computers/sensors
  • Body Hardware & Emission Control — door hinges/handles, fuel injection system, catalytic converter, EGR system

Standard Owner Protection Services

  • Trip interruption — up to $100/day for hotel, meals & transport (300km+ from home)
  • Vehicle rental assistance — $60/day, up to 5 days
  • Travel planning assistance with personalized route planner
  • 24/7/365 roadside assistance program
  • Towing up to 300km per occurrence, battery boost, tire change, winching from ditch
  • Fuel delivery & key lockout service up to $100/occurrence

Cash vs. Finance — Which Comes Out Ahead?

Paying cash means no interest and no monthly payment. Financing frees up that same cash to be invested instead. Which path actually leaves you further ahead depends on your rate, your term and your expected return — this tool walks through it.

Pay Cash

  • No interest paid over the life of the loan
  • No monthly payment or debt obligation
  • Cash is no longer available to grow elsewhere

Finance & Invest the Difference

  • Vehicle is financed at your negotiated rate
  • Cash you would have spent stays invested and compounds
  • Outcome depends on your investment return vs. your interest rate
